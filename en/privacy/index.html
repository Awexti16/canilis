Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026. The French version prevails in case of discrepancy.

In short. Canilis analyzes a few frames taken from your videos to describe your dog’s behavior. These frames go through our server and then our AI provider, Anthropic, which does not use them to train its models. The original video is deleted after the analysis unless you choose to keep it. No ads, no selling, no tracking. You can export or delete all your data from the app at any time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone (videos are recorded without sound) or payment details (handled entirely by Apple or Google). No facial recognition, no ad profiling, no cross-app tracking. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone.

3. AI processing

The app extracts 4 to 6 frames on your phone, checks their quality and sends them encrypted to our server, which forwards them to Anthropic (Claude AI model). Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use API data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time, notably for safety, before deleting it. Results are probabilistic interpretations of an animal’s behavior; no decision with legal effects on you is made automatically. Please avoid filming people, especially children.

4. Recipients

Only our technical providers acting on our instructions: Supabase Inc. (database, accounts, storage, analysis server — European Union region), Anthropic PBC (AI analysis, USA), RevenueCat Inc. (subscription management, receives an anonymous ID and purchase history, USA), Apple / Google (payment, distribution), GitHub, Inc. (this website, GitHub Pages, USA). Transfers outside the EU rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We never sell or rent your data.

5. Retention

  • Original video: deleted right after analysis; if you enable “Keep my videos”, kept for 30 days at most.
  • Account, dog profile, analyses, thumbnails, feedback, usage events and logs: as long as your account exists, permanently deleted when you delete it.
  • Subscription records at Apple, Google and RevenueCat: according to their legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may access, rectify, erase and port your data, object to or restrict processing and withdraw consent at any time. In the app: Settings → Export my data and Settings → Delete my account. For any other request, email us; we reply within one month. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

7. Security, minors, cookies

Encrypted connections, private file storage with temporary links, per-user database access rules, AI keys kept server-side only. Canilis is not intended for children under 15. The app and this website use no cookies or ad trackers.
