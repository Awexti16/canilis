Help and FAQ

Question or problem? Email support@canilis.app with your phone model and app version (Profile → Settings, bottom of the page). We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do I get a reliable analysis?

Film 5 to 10 seconds in good light with your whole dog visible (head, body and tail), keeping the phone steady, in a natural situation.

Why was my video rejected?

The app checks videos before sending anything: too dark, too short (under 2 s), too long (over 20 s) or no dog visible. Videos without a visible dog never count against your analyses.

Is sound analyzed?

No. Videos are recorded without sound and Canilis has no microphone access.

Can Canilis tell if my dog is sick?

No. Canilis is not a medical tool. When a warning sign is spotted, the app recommends seeing a vet.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Canilis → Cancel, at least 24 h before renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

New phone?

Sign in with your email, then tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen. Save your account (Profile → Account) before switching devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. All your data and files are permanently erased.
