2. What Canilis is — and is not

Canilis spots visible body signals in short videos of your dog and suggests an interpretation of its emotional state and possible intent, refined by your feedback.

Canilis is not a veterinary or medical tool. It does not diagnose and does not replace a vet or a dog trainer. Results are probabilistic and may be wrong. If you have any doubt about your dog’s health, pain or behavior, see a professional; in an emergency, contact a vet immediately.

“Playful translations” are entertainment inspired by observed signals, not your dog’s actual thoughts.
